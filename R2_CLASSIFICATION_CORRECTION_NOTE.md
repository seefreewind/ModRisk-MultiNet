# R2 derived pair-level classification correction

The R2 repair separated three quantities previously conflated by derived labels: inferential signed baseline-to-joint change, direction of absolute-magnitude change, and residual-correlation significance. Of 2,485 pairs, 1,019 had an FDR-significant signed difference; 900 attenuated in absolute magnitude and 119 enhanced. Independently, 1,100 retained FDR-significant residual correlations.

The prior derived labels conflated signed inferential change with change in absolute genetic-correlation magnitude. The earlier mutually exclusive classes are a **deprecated legacy classification**, retained only for historical audit.

**No GWAS, LDSC, partialLDSC, joint-conditioning, or MR estimates were rerun or changed.** Primary numerical genetic estimates remain unchanged. Figure 2 wording and the R2 Figure 3/4 displays follow the corrected definitions.
