## Rules
1. The 96 NEAR_NULL_CLASSIFIED pairs are described as "near-null classified" — never as
   "fully explained" or "eliminated".
2. Only the 2 STRICT_EQUIVALENT_005 pairs may be described as "statistically compatible
   with residual |rg| < 0.05" (95% CI entirely within ±0.05).
3. Attenuation is never called mediation.
4. Q heterogeneity is never described as proof of horizontal pleiotropy.
5. MR-AHC absence of clusters is reported as a result, not a failure.
6. Phase-4 results reported are the CORRECTED (positive-SMK) outputs; archived
   negative-orientation outputs are never cited.
7. All effect sizes are reported per-SD of SD-scaled exposure; OR conversions labelled per-SD.
